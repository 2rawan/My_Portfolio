# WASL — Behance upload copy

**Project title:** WASL — Accessible Transit App (UI/UX Case Study)

**Tagline:** A clearer way through the city.

**Project description:**
WASL is a self-initiated concept for an accessible transit app, designed in 2026. It makes routes, live arrivals and saved journeys easier to understand by designing around readable information and inclusive interactions.

Step-free filtering sits on the first screen, route options carry clear accessibility badges, and live arrivals show minutes, platform and lift status in words and icons, never colour alone. A dedicated settings screen lets riders adjust text size, contrast, screen-reader hints, transfer time and voice announcements.

This case study covers the challenge, research questions and assumptions, an assumption-based proto-persona, user flow, wireframes, six high-fidelity iOS screens and the design system. A usability test script is planned as the next step. No test results are claimed.
