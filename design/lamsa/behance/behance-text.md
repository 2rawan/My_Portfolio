# LAMSA — Behance copy-paste fields

**Project title:** LAMSA — Donation App UX/UI Case Study

**Tagline:** Making community giving feel personal.

**Project description:**
LAMSA is a self-initiated donation app concept shaped around trust and transparency. The case study moves from discovery and journey mapping to a simple giving flow and visible impact tracking.

The goal was to make generosity feel confident and effortless: verified organizers and clear progress build trust before the ask, a single focused screen keeps giving quick, and a personal impact view closes the loop after every gift.

This project covers research questions and assumptions to validate, a proto-persona, the user flow, low-fidelity wireframes, six high-fidelity iOS screens and a small design system built in Figma with Plus Jakarta Sans and a warm coral palette. It is a concept project, not a live product, and the assumptions shown are intended to be tested in future research.
