# DAR — Behance upload copy

**Project title:** DAR — Sustainable Furniture Marketplace (Mobile App UI/UX)

**Tagline:** Furniture shopping with confidence.

**Project description:**
DAR is a self-initiated concept project: a mobile marketplace for considered, sustainable furniture. Buying furniture online is a leap of faith, because shoppers can't judge scale, material or fit from a photo. DAR explores how clear product discovery, useful product details and an AR room preview can help people imagine each piece at home before they buy.

This case study walks through the challenge, research questions and assumptions to validate, a proto-persona, the user flow, low-fidelity wireframes and six final iOS screens: Home, Browse, Product details, AR preview, Cart and Order confirmed. It closes with the design system: a warm palette of Walnut, Olive and Sand, Playfair Display for headings and Inter for interface text.

The research sections frame questions and assumptions, not validated findings. Designed in Figma.

**Tools used:** Figma

**Creative fields:** UI/UX, Mobile Design, Interaction Design

**Tags:** UI design, UX design, mobile app, iOS, furniture, e-commerce, augmented reality, sustainable design, information architecture, wireframing

**Visibility:** Everyone
